In complex engineering projects, understanding an individual interface is only part of the picture. One OOC may connect to several others, and each of those connections can lead to further dependencies across the architecture.

With the new **Chain Analysis** feature in SEC-Hub, you can explore these connections directly from the N² grid and see how interfaces extend beyond a single OOC.

## Go beyond the first connection

The N² grid provides a structured overview of interfaces between OOCs. Chain Analysis takes that visibility a step further.

Select an OOC to explore its interface network up to **two layers deep**:

- **Layer 1:** Identify OOCs directly connected to your selected OOC through interfaces.
- **Layer 2:** Discover OOCs connected to those first-layer OOCs, revealing relationships that may not be immediately obvious.

Instead of examining each interface in isolation, you can follow the connections and build a clearer picture of how OOCs relate to one another.

## Understand the wider impact

An interface rarely exists in isolation. A change involving one OOC may have implications for others, including those without a direct interface to the original OOC.

Chain Analysis helps engineering teams spot potential dependencies earlier, ask better questions during design reviews, and identify where further impact assessment may be needed.

Whether you are evaluating a proposed change, reviewing an architecture, or investigating a complex interface network, the feature gives you a more connected view of the engineering model.

## Turn complexity into clarity

Large N² grids can contain many interfaces. Finding the relevant connections manually can take time, particularly when the relationships span multiple OOCs.

Chain Analysis makes it easier to focus on one starting point and explore the surrounding network in a structured way. It helps teams:

- Trace direct and indirect connections between OOCs.
- Discover dependencies across two interface layers.
- Support more informed design and change discussions.
- Communicate how different parts of an architecture are connected.

## Explore the connections that matter

With Chain Analysis, the N² grid becomes more than an overview of interfaces. It becomes a starting point for exploring how OOCs are connected across your architecture.

**Select an OOC, follow its interfaces, and see the bigger picture.**
