Engineering teams need a clear view of their system architecture, but that structure is most useful when it can support the tools they already work with. With SEC-Hub's **Jama Connect integration**, you can export the OOCs defined in SEC-Hub to Jama Connect, bringing your architecture into your requirements management environment.

## From architecture to requirements workflows

SEC-Hub helps teams organize complex solutions into OOCs and understand how they fit together. Jama Connect provides an environment for managing requirements and related engineering work.

Exporting OOCs gives teams a practical way to carry their system structure from SEC-Hub into Jama Connect, rather than recreating it manually from scratch.

## Reduce duplicate work

When architecture and requirements are managed in different tools, transferring information can become a repetitive task. The integration helps streamline that handoff:

- Define and organize your OOCs in SEC-Hub.
- Export those OOCs to Jama Connect.
- Use the exported structure as a starting point for requirements-related work in Jama Connect.
- Give teams working across both tools a more consistent view of the system breakdown.

The result is less time spent repeating structural information and more time spent on engineering decisions.

## Connect teams around a shared structure

System architects and requirements engineers often work in different environments. By making SEC-Hub OOCs available in Jama Connect, teams can build on a common architectural starting point and make discussions about scope, ownership, and requirements more precise.

## Make your engineering workflow more connected

The Jama Connect integration is another step toward connecting architecture work with the wider engineering process. Start with a clear OOC structure in SEC-Hub, then export it to Jama Connect to support the next stage of your workflow.
